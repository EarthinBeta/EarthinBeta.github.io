Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers the Earth in Beta creator application and this informational website. The application is operated for the Earth in Beta channel and its authorized operator. Privacy contact: adamantiumblando@gmail.com.

1. Purpose and access

The application supports the creation, upload, scheduling and status tracking of the operator's own videos. It uses Google APIs only after the account owner grants the relevant permissions. This website does not itself initiate Google sign-in or collect Google passwords.

2. Information used

  • Authorization information: OAuth access and refresh tokens, granted permissions and connection state needed to maintain the authorized connection.
  • YouTube information: channel identifiers and basic channel information; video files and metadata supplied for upload, including titles, descriptions, visibility and publication settings; video identifiers, links and processing or publication status.
  • Selected script documents: where the Google Drive workflow is enabled and authorized, document identifiers, names, text and version information needed to import and track the operator's selected scripts.
  • Workflow records: approved scripts, episode identifiers, generated videos, timestamps, job states and operational or error records needed for production, review and duplicate prevention.

The application uses the permissions actually granted by the operator; it is not a service for accessing other people's channels or unrelated files.

3. How information is used

Information is used to import approved scripts, produce the requested content, carry out authorized uploads or publication settings, verify results, prevent duplicate processing and troubleshoot failures. Google API data is not sold or used for targeted advertising, credit decisions or the training of generalized artificial-intelligence models.

4. Storage and access

Production files, authorization credentials and workflow records are maintained on the operator's Windows production system and in connected services configured for the workflow. Submitted scripts and job states may also be held by the private script-receiving service that transfers approved work to that system. Google Drive retains its source documents and YouTube retains uploaded content under the account's settings.

Access is limited to the operator and services required to perform the requested workflow. Credentials are not part of this public website or its public source repository. Operational records and content are retained while needed to run, review and maintain the workflow; the operator can remove local records and files.

5. Sharing and connected services

Google receives the API requests and content needed for the enabled Drive and YouTube operations. The private script-receiving service processes submitted scripts and job states. When the operator intentionally uses ChatGPT, Claude or other production services for script development or content generation, that submitted material is processed under the selected service's own terms and settings. This does not grant permission to transmit unrelated Google account data to those services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law. Google API data is otherwise used and transferred only for the application features described here and consistently with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

6. Revocation and deletion

You can revoke Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation stops further authorized Google API access but does not automatically delete existing local files, Drive documents or uploaded YouTube videos.

Contact adamantiumblando@gmail.com to request deletion of application-held scripts, job records or credentials. The operator will verify the request and remove the associated application-held copies, subject to any applicable retention requirements. Content held in Google Drive or YouTube can also be managed or deleted directly in the relevant Google account. Deleting a local record does not itself delete a published video.

7. Website and hosting

This static website includes no account forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers or custom tracking cookies. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as explained in the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are governed by their respective services.

8. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change. Where a change requires additional Google permissions or new uses of Google data, the operator must authorize those permissions or uses before they begin.

Questions and requests: adamantiumblando@gmail.com.
